# Custom character set

- Ideally at least 16 characters per set.

## DOTA

- Bounty Hunter: When I stop on a space with exactly one other racer, I steal 1 point chip from them. Cups are safe.
- Spirit Breaker: When I pass other racer, they have to roll a dice. If it is 1, they are tripped.
- Earthshaker: I can skip my main move to trip other racers in my space, and move 2 space for each racers I tripped. Cannot be used on the Start space.
- Tidehunter: Each time I tripped, I roll a dice. If it's 4 or larger, I stand up immediately.
- Templar Assassin: I ignore the first 3 trips I received.
- Anti-mage: I can skip my main move and _warp_ to any space within 3 spaces ahead.
- Faceless Void: Once per round, I can trip everyone within 5 spaces near me.
- Silencer: Once per round, I can disable the ability of all racer for one turn (It mean other racer's next turn, they can only roll for main move).
- Kunkka: After my main move, I can _warp_ back to the space I was before my main move.
- Omniknight: Other racer within 3 spaces near me get -2 to their main move.
- Orge Magi: I roll two d3s and multiply them, so I move 1 to 9.
- Morphling: I have the power of any racer currently last. If there's a tie, I choose.
- Alchemist: I received double the points from finish cup and star space.
- Legion Commander: Whenever a racer shares my space, I can shout DUEL! We roll our dice and whoever rolls highest get +1 permanently to their main move. I win ties.
- Oracle: At the start of my first turn, I predict which racer will trip first. If I'm right, I get 3 points.
- Storm Spirit: I roll a d4. Once per race, I can roll a d20 instead.
- Bloodseeker: I get +1 to my main move for each other racer currently tripped.
- Clockwerk: Before my main move, I push every racer 1 space away from me.
- Pudge: I can skip my main move to warp any racer to my space and trip them.
- Techies: Every space I stop on becomes a TRIP space.
- Chaos Knight: I roll a d20, and get -9 to my main move. It can take me backwards.
- Abaddon: Whenever another racer trips, I can help them up at once. If I do, I move 3.
- Bristleback: If I trip, I also trip all racers within 3 spaces near me.
- Drow Ranger: I use a d4. If no other racer within 3 spaces near me, I use a d8 instead.
- Night Stalker: I get +2 to my main move on my odd turns, and -1 on my even turns.
- Slark: I get +1 to my main move for every silver cup, and +2 for every gold cup.
- Ember Spirit: I can skip my main move to move 2 for each other racer within 3 spaces of me.
- Earth Spirit: Before my main move, I can kick one racer on my space 3 spaces forward or backward.
